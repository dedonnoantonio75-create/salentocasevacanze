+10+7

Description

Between Gallipoli and Lido Conchiglie, on the Ionian side of the Salento peninsula, Casa Orsa Mono in Rivabella is about 150 metres from the sea and its welcoming beach. Restaurants, pizzerias, an equipped free beach, lidos and a popular water park are within walking distance, and the historic centre of Gallipoli, the “Beautiful City”, is 5 minutes away by car. The studio home offers a kitchen, a double bedroom, one bathroom, washing machine and TV. Sleeps 2.
