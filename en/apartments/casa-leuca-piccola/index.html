Casa Leuca Piccola

📍 Barbarano del Capo · Salento, Puglia

+16+13
👥 5 guests🛏️ 2 bedrooms🛁 1 bathroom

Description

In the Salento countryside, in the small village of Barbarano del Capo, Casa Leuca Piccola is ideal for families looking for a totally relaxing holiday. The splendid historic complex of “Leuca Piccola”, typical trattorias and the old town centre are within walking distance; the beaches of Torre Vado, Pescoluse and the “Maldives of Salento” are just 5 minutes away by car, and Leuca, Gallipoli, Otranto and Lecce are easy day trips. The house has a large living room, a spacious fully equipped kitchen, a double bedroom, a twin bedroom, one bathroom, TV and washing machine. Sleeps up to 5.
