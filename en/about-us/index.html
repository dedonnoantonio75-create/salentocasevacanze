About Us

Salento Case Vacanze brings together professionals with many years of experience in tourism, hospitality and guest services.

Our job is to make your holiday memorable, with options for every budget and every need: from planning the trip — choosing the right destination and the right holiday home — to organising experiences and services that help you get the best out of the region and its people.

Not your usual property manager, but a project combining years of experience in tourism with a shared passion: making Salento known and giving everyone the chance to experience its most authentic side and its most beautiful places.

We start from here — we start from you — to help you choose the best possible options for your holiday. Whether low cost or a seafront home, surrounded by greenery or close to the nightlife: many different options, for all needs and all pockets, with the services you need and complete peace of mind — the kind that comes from relying on people who truly know the trade and the territory.

Ready for your holiday in Salento?

Check availability of our holiday homes or write to us: we will help you choose the perfect one.
